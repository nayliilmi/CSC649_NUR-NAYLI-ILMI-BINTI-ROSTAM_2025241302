1.2 Chosen Specialized Technology

In this project, a Rule-Based Expert System is selected as the specialized technology to enhance the existing UAPS ePelekat Kenderaan system. The use of the technology is to predefined IF-THEN rules to evaluate application information and supporting documents based on several conditions. This approach allows the proposed system to simply identify missing information, detect invalid document formats and provide trigger notifications to users during the application process. The rule-based feature will be integrated into mutlti-step application form as a tabs format to help users complete their applications accurately.
